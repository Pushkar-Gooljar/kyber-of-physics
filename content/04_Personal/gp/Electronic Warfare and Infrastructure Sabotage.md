# Electromagnetic Spectrum Warfare
>[!info] GNSS Jamming 
>Overpowering legitimate  Global Navigation Satellite System (GNSS) signals with high-power radio frequency noise, rendering GPS receivers blind.

>[!info] GPS Spoofing
>False GPS satellite signals are transmitted to deceive receivers into calculating incorrect location or time corrdinates.

>[!danger] Impacts 
>- Spikes in wide-are state-sponsored spoofing throughout 2025 and 2026 have severely disrupted commercial aviation and maritime shipping in the Baltic, Sea, Black Sea, Straight of Hormuz, and Eastern Mediterranean.
>- In 2025, during regional escalation in the Middle East, GPS spoofing in the Straight of Hormuz forced **nearly 1,000 vessels per day** to alter transit schedules **cutting traffic by 20%**

>[!danger] Indirect Impacts
>Beyond navigation, GPS spoofing alters the hyper-precise atomic timestamps required to synchronise telecom networks, financial markets and electricity grids.

# Counter-Space and Satellite Interdiction
>[!info] Anti-Satellite Missiles
>Land or air launched ballistic missiles designed to collide with satellites in Low Earth Orbit (LEO) at hypersonic speeds

>[!danger] Impact
>- The use of kinetic ASATs generates thousands of pieces of space debris.
>- This orbital debris can destroy or damage neighbouring civilian, scientific and communication satellites, effectively rendering specific orbital altitudes unusable for decades.

# Subsurface Marine Sabotage 
>[!info] Importance of Undersea fibre-optic cables
>They carry approximately **99% of all intercontinental data traffic**

>[!info] Anchor dragging tactic
>- State-backed actors employ commercial or state-owned cargo ships and fishing vessels to secretly drag heavy anchors along the seabed for dozens of miles.
>- This tactic severs submarine communication while providing the plausible deniability of an "accidental maritime event"

>[!abstract] Examples
>- **2024 Baltic Sea Disruptions:** The BCS East-West Interlink (connecting Lithuania and Sweden) and the C-Lion1 cable (Connecting Finland and Germany) were cut simultaneously.
>- Western intelligence identified the Chinese cargo ship **Yi Peng 3** dragging its anchor over the cables while suspected of operating under Russian coordination.
