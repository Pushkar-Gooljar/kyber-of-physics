>[!info] Core Concept
>Increasing, physical, mechanical, or perceptual defence between a combatant and their target bypasses the brain's innate evolutionary resistance to taking human life.

>[!warning]
>However, modern high-definition warfare introduces the **Distance Paradox**: physical distance makes pulling the trigger easier, but visual intimacy creates long term moral injury.

> [!cite] Key Empirical Finding
> A 2017 study in the *Journal of Experimental Social Psychology* (Rutchick et al.) demonstrated that technologically facilitated remoteness increases willingness to inflict harm and significantly lowers immediate negative emotional response during the execution of the act.


## The 3 Vectors of Distance
| Vector                      | Description                              | Example                                  | Psychological Impact                                              |
| :-------------------------- | :--------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Physical Distance**       | Geographical separation in space.        | Firing artillery vs. bayonet combat.     | High physical distance lowers the emotional barrier to near zero. |
| **Mechanical Distance**     | Technological buffers mediating the act. | Thermal screens, scopes, HUDs, buttons.  | Targets are reduced to blips, thermal silhouettes, or pixels.     |
| **Social / Moral Distance** | Dehumanization and cultural separation.  | Ideological framing, class/racial slurs. | Reframes the enemy as a threat, non-human, or objective.          |
## Distance Paradox
* **Classic PTSD:** Rooted in personal fear, survival threat, and bodily danger.
* **Moral Injury:** Rooted in guilt, shame, and betrayal of deeply held moral codes after perpetrating or witnessing horrific acts.
* *Finding:* Drone pilots suffer from high rates of **Moral Injury** rather than traditional threat-based PTSD because they operate without personal physical risk.

> [!warning] "The Commute Trauma"
> Drone operators face severe psychological friction caused by rapid cognitive shifts. An operator may execute a strike on a target in a warzone, watch the aftermath, and **45 minutes later be driving home in suburban traffic to eat dinner with their family**. This eliminates the military "decompression period" traditionally experienced on troop ships or bases.

## The PlayStation Mentality and Dehumanisation
>[!info] Gamification
>Interfaces utilising joysticks, monitors, and target crosshairs can trigger a "video game" mentality, detaching the operator from real world consequences (P.W. Singer, Wired For War)

>[!info] Linguistic Distancing
>Military terminology used to reduce emotional attachment
>- "Bug splat" : Slang for the thermal image of a target hit by a missile
>- "Kinetic strike" : euphemism for lethal bombing
