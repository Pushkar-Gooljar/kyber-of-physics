Electrons emit photons when transitioning between energy levels. ^Y2ENV8I8

Photon energy is equal to difference between energy levels (E=hf) ^AJTTxGeJ

Photon energy is related to frequency. ^jDlZuLLg

Therefore electron energy levels must be discrete. ^D40fAtBa

Therefore difference between electron energy levels must be discrete. ^r1xsibCP

Discrete frequencies on spectrum mean that photon energies are discrete. ^w1WKHCOL
