## 5. Who is responsible? (from your other notes)

- **Carbon Majors (IND-S001, 2024)**: about **80%** of fossil-fuel and cement CO₂ since 2016 comes from **57** producers. → *Responsibility is concentrated, not individual.*
- **EU supply-chain due diligence directive (IND-N010, 2024)**: large companies must check their supply chains for environmental harm.
- **South Africa's Climate Change Act (IND-N011, 2025)** introduced binding carbon budgets, while the **US EPA moved to repeal power-plant rules (IND-N012, 2026)**. → *Action depends on who governs.*
- **Chile's climate coordination (STA-N016)**: trust in coordination across government agencies boosts public confidence in climate targets.

## 7. Well-established facts your note is missing (not in your notes; verify the exact numbers before memorising)

**Human activity and disasters**
- **IPCC Sixth Assessment Report (2021–23)**: it is "unequivocal" that humans have warmed the planet; heatwaves and heavy rainfall have become more frequent and intense.
- **2024 was the hottest year on record** and the first calendar year more than **1.5°C** above pre-industrial levels (Copernicus).
- **Pakistan floods (2022)**: about a third of the country under water, around **1,700** deaths and about **$30 billion** in damage and losses; Pakistan produces under 1% of global emissions. → *Those who pollute least suffer most.*
- **Valencia floods (October 2024)**: over **200** deaths. Scientists linked the extreme rainfall to warmer seas, but warnings came late and building on floodplains made it worse. → *Disasters are both natural and man-made.*

**Problems that were solved**
- **Montreal Protocol (1987)**: phased out ozone-destroying CFCs; the ozone layer is on track to recover by around the 2060s. → *The strongest proof that global problems can be solved*, when alternatives exist and a few producers are involved.
- **Renewables overtaking coal**: solar and wind are now the cheapest new electricity in most of the world, and in 2025 renewables overtook coal in global power generation for the first time (Ember, first half of 2025).
- **Costa Rica**: almost all its electricity comes from renewables, and forest cover has recovered to over half the country after heavy deforestation in the 1980s.

**Biodiversity and plastics**
- **WWF Living Planet Report (2024)**: monitored wildlife populations fell by an average of **73%** since 1970.
- **Kunming-Montreal Framework (2022)**: countries agreed to protect **30%** of land and sea by 2030 ("30x30").
- **Plastic bag bans**: Rwanda (2008) and Kenya (2017, among the world's strictest). → *Small, poorer states can enforce bold rules.*

**Individual responsibility**
- **The richest 10%** of people cause roughly **half** of global consumption emissions (Oxfam / Chancel). → *"Individual responsibility" is really the responsibility of wealthy individuals.*
- **Greta Thunberg's school strikes (from 2018)** grew into global youth climate strikes involving millions in 2019. → *Young people can shift the agenda, but not make policy.*
