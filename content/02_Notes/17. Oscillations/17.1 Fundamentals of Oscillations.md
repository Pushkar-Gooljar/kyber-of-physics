## Key Definitions
>[!info] Displacement, $x$
>- The distance of an oscillator from its **equilibrium position** in a specific direction.

>[!info] Amplitude, $x_{o}$
>- The **maximum displacement** from the equilibrium position.

>[!info] Period, $T$
>- The time taken to complete one full oscillation.

>[!info] Frequency, $f$
>- The number of oscillations per unit time.
