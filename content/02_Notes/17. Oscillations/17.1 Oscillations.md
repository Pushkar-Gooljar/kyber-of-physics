# Oscillations

>[!info] Oscillation
>An oscillation is the repetitive variation with time of the displacement of an object about the equilibrium position

## Key Terms
![Example: A simple pendulum.|600](https://cdn.savemyexams.com/cdn-cgi/image/f=auto,width=1920/https://cdn.savemyexams.com/uploads/2022/05/4-1-1-graphing-oscillations_sl-physics-rn.png)

>[!info] Equilibrium position ($x=0$)
>The position where there is no resultant force acting on the object

>[!info] Displacement ($x$)
>Distance from equilibrium position in a given direction 

>[!info] Amplitude ($x_{0}$)
>Maximum displacement from equilibrium position 

>[!info] Period ($T$)
>Time for one complete oscillation 

>[!info] Frequency ($f$)
>Number of complete oscillations per unit time

>[!info] Angular Frequency $\omega$)
>Angular frequency = $2\pi \times frequency$

>[!danger] Phase Difference ($\phi$)
>Difference in the stage of oscillation between two points in radians
