## Definition 
>[!info] Conditions: Simple Harmonic Motion
>A motion is said to be **Simple Harmonic** if:
>- The acceleration is directly proportional to the displacement.
>- The acceleration and displacement are in opposite directions.


## Equation
Mathematically,
$$
\large
\begin{align}
a &\propto -x \\
a &= -kx \\
a &=-\omega^2x
\end{align}
$$
The equation defining **Simple Harmonic Motion** is:
$$
\large
\boxed{a=-\omega^2x}
$$
>[!tip] Key Point: Negative Sign
>The negative sign int he equation indicates that the acceleration, and therefore the restoring force, is always in the **opposite direction** to the displacement.
>For example,
>- If the body is moving right, the force pulls the body to the left.
>- If the body is moving left, the force pulls the body to the right.
