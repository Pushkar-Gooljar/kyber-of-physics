## Displacement-Time Equation
The equation $a=-\omega^2x$ can be written as a second order differential equation by substituting acceleration, $a$ by the second derivative of displacement, $x$:
$$
\large
\begin{align}
a&=-\omega^2x \\
\frac{d^2x}{dt^2} &= -\omega^2x
\end{align}
$$
A solution of this differential equation is:
$$
\large
x = x_{o}\sin \omega t
