## Displacement
>[!math] Derivation of Displacement ($x$)
>- The displacement $x$ of the shadow on the screen is equal to the distance $DC$.
>- From the right angled triangle formed by the radius $r$ and angle $\theta$:
>$$\large x = DC = r\sin \theta$$
>- By definition, angular velocity $\omega$ is the rate of change of angular displacement $\theta$:
>$$\large\omega = \frac{\theta}{t} \implies \theta = \omega t$$
>- Substituting $\theta = \omega t$ into $x=r\sin \theta$:
>$$\large x = r\sin \omega t$$
>- Since the radius of the circle $r$ represents the maximum displacement from the equilibrium position, it is the amplitude $x_{0}$:
>$$\large x = x_{0}\sin \omega t$$
