# 15.2 Ideal Gas Equation of State
## Ideal Gases
An ideal gas is a theoretical gas that perfectly obeys they gas laws under all conditions.
>[!info] Definition: Ideal Gas
> - An ideal gas is a gas which obeys the relationship $pV \propto T$
> - For all values of $p$, $V$, and $T$, where $T$ is the thermodynamic temperature.

## Equation of State
>[!math] Macroscopic Form (Using Moles)
>$$\large pV = nRT$$
>
>*Where:*
>
>   - $p$ : Pressure ($\text{Pa}$)
>   - $V$ : Volume ($\text{m}^3$)
>   - $n$ : Amount of substance ($\text{mol}$)
>   - $R$ : Molar gas constant ($8.31 \text{ J K}^{-1} \text{ mol}^{-1}$)
>   - $T$ : Thermodynamic temperature ($\text{K}$)

>[!math] Microscopic Form (Using Molecules)
>$$\large pV = NkT$$
>
>*Where*:
>   - $p$ : Pressure ($\text{Pa}$)
>   - $V$ : Volume ($\text{m}^3$)
>   - $N$ : Total number of molecules
>   - $k$ : Boltzmann Constant ($1.38 \times 10^{-23} \text{ J K}^{-1}$)
>   - $T$ : Thermodynamic temperature ($\text{K}$)

>[!important] Boltzmann Constant ($k$)
>$$\large k = \frac{R}{N_{A}}$$
