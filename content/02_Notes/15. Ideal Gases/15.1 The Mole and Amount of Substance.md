# 15.1 The Mole and Amount of Substance
>[!info] Definition: The Mole
>- Amount of substance containing a number of particles equal to the **Avogadro Constant** ($N_{A}$)

>[!info] Definition: Avogadro Constant ($N_{A}$)
>- Number of particles per unit amount of substance 
>- Is equal to $6.02 \times 10^{23}$

>[!math] Calculating Number of Moles of Substance
>You can calculate the number of moles ($n$) using either the number of molecules ($N$) or the mass of the substance ($m$):
>$$\large n=\frac{N}{N_{A}}$$
>$$\large n=\frac{m}{M_{r}}$$
>
>Where:
>- $N$: Total number of particles.
>- $N_{A}$: Avogadro's constant ($6.02 \times 10^{23}$).
>- $m$: Total mass of substance (g or kg).
>- $M_{r}$: Molar mass (mass of one mole of substance in $\mathrm{gmol^{-1}}$ or $\mathrm{kgmol^{-1}}$).
