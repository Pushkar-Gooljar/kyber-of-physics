# $E_{k_{max}}$ against $f$
>[!info] Maximum kinetic energy against frequency
>$$E_{K_{max}}=hf-\Phi$$
>- **Gradient:** Planck Constant ($h$)
>- **$x$-intercept:** Threshold frequency ($f_{0}$)
>- **$y$-intercept:** $-(\text{Work Function})$ ($-\Phi$)
>
>![Graph of maximum kinetic energy of photoelectrons against frequency of EM radiation|500](ekmax-frequency.png)

# $E_{k_{max}}$ against $\frac{1}{\lambda}$

>[!info] Maximum kinetic energy against $\frac{1}{\text{Wavelength}}$
>$$E_{K_{max}}=hc\left( \frac{1}{\lambda} \right)-\Phi$$
>- **Gradient:** $\text{Planck constant} \times\text{speed of light}$ ($hc$)
>- **$x$-intercept:** $\frac{1}{\text{Threshold wavelength}}$($\frac{1}{\lambda_{0}}$)
>- **$y$-intercept:** $-(\text{Work Function})$ ($-\Phi$)
>
>![Graph of maximum kinetic energy of photoelectrons against inverse of wavelength of EM radiation|500](ekmax-1-lambda.png)

# $E_{k_{max}}$ against $\lambda$

>[!info] Maximum kinetic energy against wavelength
>$$E_{K_{max}}=\frac{hc}{\lambda}-\Phi$$
>- **$x$-intercept:** Threshold wavelength ($\lambda_{0}$)
>- **Horizontal asymptote:** $-(\text{Work Function})$ ($-\Phi$)
>
>![Graph of maximum kinetic energy of photoelectrons against wavelength of EM radiation|500](ekmax-lambda.png)

# $V_{s}$ against $f$

>[!info] Stopping potential against frequency
>$$V_{s}=\left( \frac{h}{e} \right)f -\frac{\Phi}{e}$$
>- **Gradient:** $\frac{\text{Planck Constant}}{\text{electronic charge}}$ ($\large \frac{h}{e}$)
>- **$x$-intercept:** Threshold frequency ($f_{0}$)
>- **$y$-intercept:** $-\left( \frac{\text{Work Function}}{\text{electronic charge}} \right)$ ($\large -\frac{\Phi}{e}$)
>
>![Graph of stopping potential against frequency|500](stopping-potential-frequency.png)

>[!math] Derivation
>$$V_{s}=\frac{E_{k_{max}}}{e}$$
>$$V_{s}=\frac{hf-\Phi}{e}$$
>$$V_{s}=\left( \frac{h}{e} \right)f-\frac{\Phi}{e}$$

# $\lambda$ against $p$

>[!info] de Broglie wavelength against momentum
>$$\lambda = \frac{h}{p}$$
>- **Relationship:** Inverse relationship (hyperbolic curve)
>- **Asymptotes:** Both axes ($\lambda = 0$ and $p = 0$) act as asymptotes
>
>![Graph of de Broglie wavelength against momentum|500](de-broglie-wavelength-momentum.png)

# $p$ against $\frac{1}{\lambda}$

>[!info] Momentum against inverse de Broglie wavelength
>$$p = h\left(\frac{1}{\lambda}\right)$$
>- **Gradient:** Planck Constant ($h$)
>- **Intercept:** Passes through the origin ($(0,0)$)
>
>![Graph of momentum against inverse de Broglie wavelength|500](momentum-inverse-wavelength.png)
