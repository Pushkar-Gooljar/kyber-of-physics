# Photons
>[!info] What is a Photon?
>A photon is a quantum of electromagnetic radiation

# Photon Energy
The energy of a photon having a frequency $f$ is given by:
$$
E=hf
$$
Where $h$ is Planck's Constant ($6.63 \times 10^{-34}\text{ m}^2\text{kg}\text{ s}^{-1}$)

>[!info] Photon Energy using Frequency
>$$E=hf$$

>[!info] Photon Energy using Wavelength 
>$$E=\frac{hc}{\lambda}$$

>[!important] Insight
>More energetic photons have a higher frequency and a lower wavelength (Recall that $c=f\lambda$)
