# Using Ultrasound in Medical Imaging

> [!success] Model Answer
> **Explain the principles of using ultrasound to obtain diagnostic information about internal body structures.**
> - A pulse of ultrasound is emitted by a transducer.
> - Ultrasound wave is reflected at boundaries.
> - Reflected pulse is detected by the transducer.
> - Reflected wave is processed and displayed.
> - Time delay between generation and detection gives information about depth. 
> - Intensity of reflected wave gives information about the nature of the boundary.
> - A coupling gel is used to minimise reflection at the skin.
> - Ultrasound waves are generated and detected by a quartz crystal.
> - Degree of reflection depends upon specific acoustic impedances of the two media at the boundary.

>[!important] Why are pulses used
>- So that there is no interference between transmitted and reflected waves.
>- A single crystal is used to generate and detect ultrasounds in the transducer, therefore it cannot simultaneously generate and detect ultrasound. It must send a pulse and wait for detection before sending another pulse.
