### Generation and Detection of Ultrasound (Piezo-electric effect)
>[!info] 9702/44/M/J/25 Q10(a) (Generation)
>**Describe how the piezoelectric crystal in a transducer generates ultrasound waves for use in medical diagnosis. [3]**
>- Applying a p.d. across the crystal causes it to distort.
>- Applying an alternating p.d. across the crystal causes it to vibrate.
>- When the frequency of applied p.d. equals the natural frequency of the crystal, the crystal resonates.
>- Natural frequency of crystal is in ultrasound range.

>[!info] 9702/41/O/N/25 Q10(b) (Detection)
>**Explain how ultrasound waves are detected. [3]**
>- A piezoelectric crystal is used.
>- Ultrasound waves cause the crystal to vibrate.
>- Vibrations of crystal cause induced e.m.f. across crystal which is detected

>[!info] 9702/41/O/N/22 Q7(b) (Crystal Interaction with Air)
>**An alternating voltage is applied to a piezoelectric crystal in air.  Explain what happens to the air surrounding the crystal. [3]**
>- Alternating p.d. makes the crystal vibrate.
>- Vibrations of the crystal causes air to vibrate.
>- Frequency is in ultrasound range

### Specific Acoustic Impedance & Reflection (Diagnostic Principles)
>[!info] 9702/41/O/N/25 Q10(a)
>**Define specific acoustic impedance [2]**
>- Product of density of medium
>- and speed of ultrasound in the medium

>[!tip] Extra
>**What is represented by specific acoustic impedance of a medium?**
>- It describes how much resistance an ultrasound wave encounters as it passes through the medium
