>[!info] Definition: Attenuation
>- Reduction of energy due to the absorption of ultrasound as it travels through a medium.

>[!math] 
>The intensity $I$ of ultrasound decreases with distance $x$, according to the equation:
>$$I=I_{0}e^{-\mu x}$$
>
>**Where:**
>- $I$=Intensity of incident beam ($\text{W m}^{-2}$)
>- $I$=Intensity of the reflected beam ($\text{W m}^{-2}$)
>- $\mu$=Absorption coefficient ($\text{m}^{-1}$)
>- $x$=Distance travelled through the material ($\text{m}$)
