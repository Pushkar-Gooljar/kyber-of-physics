# Piezoelectric Crystal
>[!info] Definition 
>A piezoelectric crystal is a crystalline solid such as quartz that deforms when a potential difference is applied across it.

![Deformation of a quartz crystal when a p.d. is applied across it|400](https://www.audiowell.com/uploadfile/image/20200807/1596789204945505.jpg)

# Generation of Ultrasounds
## Workings of a piezoelectric crystal
>[!concept] Setup
>![Piezoelectric crystal is connected to an A.C. power supply|500](piezoelectric_crystal_off.svg)
>- A piezoelectric crystal (quartz) consists of silicon and oxygen atoms arranged in a 3D tetrahedral structure.
>- Since oxygen is more electronegative than silicon, the oxygen atoms have a partial negative charge and the silicon atoms have a partial positive charge

>[!concept] Contraction
>![Piezoelectric crystal contracts|500](piezoelectric_crystal_contract.svg)
>**When the top terminal is negative and the bottom terminal is positive**
>- Like charges repel and unlike charges attract
>- The positive silicon atoms are attracted to the top plate
>- The negative oxygen atoms are attracted to the bottom plate
>- This causes the crystal structure to stretch, leading to it contracting horizontally
>- This creates a region of rarefaction in the sound wave


>[!concept] Expansion
>![Piezoelectric crystal expands|500](piezoelectric_crystal_expand.svg)
>**When the top plate is positive and the bottom plate is negative**
>- Like charges repel and unlike charges attract
>- The positive silicon atoms are repelled from the top plate
>- The negative oxygen atoms are repelled from the bottom plate
>- This causes the crystal structure to compress, leading to it expanding horizontally
>- This creates a region of compression in the sound wave

## How is the ultrasound wave produced
- The piezoelectric crystal expands and contracts at the frequency of the applied A.C.
- When the frequency of the A.C. equals the natural frequency of the piezoelectric crystal, the crystal resonates.
- The natural frequency of the crystal is in the ultrasound wave
- This leads to the generation of ultrasound waves from the compressions and rarefactions caused by the crystal

>[!success] Model Exam Answer
>**Describe how a piezoelectric crystal in a transducer generates ultrasound waves for use in medical diagnosis.**
>- An alternating p.d. is applied across the crystal causing it to vibrate.
>- When the frequency of applied p.d. equals the natural frequency of the crystal, the crystal resonates.
>- Natural frequency of the crystal is in the ultrasound range.
