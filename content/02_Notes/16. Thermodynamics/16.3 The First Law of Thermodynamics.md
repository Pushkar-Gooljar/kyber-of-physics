# The First Law of Thermodynamics
>[!info] The First Law of Thermodynamics
>**Increase** in internal energy = work done **on** the system + energy transferred **to** the system by heating

> [!math] The First Law of Thermodynamics
> $$\Delta U = q + W$$
> Where:
> *   $\Delta U$ = Increase in internal energy of the system ($\text{J}$)
> *   $q$ = Thermal energy transferred (heating) **to** the system ($\text{J}$)
> *   $W$ = Work done **on** the system ($\text{J}$)

>[!important] Increase in Internal Energy
>- Internal energy increases 
>- Thermal energy is **supplied to** the system: $+q$
>- Work is done **on** the system: $+W$
>- Since work is done on the system, gas is compressed therefore $-\Delta V$

>[!important] Decrease in Internal Energy
>- Internal energy decreases.
>- Thermal energy is **removed from** the system: $-q$
>- Work is done **by** the system: $-W$
>- Since work is done by the system, gas expands therefore: $+\Delta V$

>[!warning]
>The First Law of Thermodynamics is defined in terms of increase in internal energy and work done **on** system.
