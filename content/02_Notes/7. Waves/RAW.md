# Eatable Supernova 

## Prep

### Tomatoes
- For Ramen: 1-2 diced tomatoes (assuming 48 mm x 60 mm tomatoes)
- For Salad: 1 diced tomato (assuming 48 mm x 60 mm tomatoes)
### Bell Pepper
- Roughly same amount when diced as the diced tomatoes used for the ramen
- May be unicolor or half green/half yellow

### Tofu
- One standard block diced

### Chilli
- 1-2 sliced thinly 

### Jalapeno 
- 1-2 sliced thinly (only 1 if chilli also used)
### Peanut butter
- About 1-3 scoops using a medium sized kitchen knife directly from peanut butter container
### Miscellaneous 
- Diced english cucumber (half as ramen tomato)
- Diced zuccini (same amount as ramen tomato)
- Minced onion (half a medium to big onion)
- Spring onion / leek (variable)
- Grated carrot (1/3 tomato ramen tomato amount)
- Violet cabbage (minced finely, about same amount as onion)



## Step 1
- add everything to a pan (except salad tomatoes)
- Add black bean sauce or soy sauce
- Grind some black pepper into the mix
- Small amount of aromat
- grate slight amout of nutmeg 
- Turn on heat on medium high and use a wooden spatula to continuously stir/mix the ingredients 
- Once the zuccini becomes translucent and tomato juices leak out, turn off heat

## Step 2
- boil ramen noodles for 2 minutes (DO NOT ADD ANYTHING BUT WATER)
- dump the water using strainer and wash with cold water to stop cooking

## Step 3
- Turn heat back on to medium/high
- keep stirring until it starts sticking to the bottom
- Add pasta sauce and stir to blend in
- Add mozarella until noticable stringing on stirring 
- add a reasonable amount of boiled chickpeas

## Step 4
- add ramen to the pan and stir to coat the ramen to obtain an almost homogenous mixture
- Add the instant ramen spice packet and stir some more
- turn off the heat and close the pan with a lid
- vigorously shake to mix everything evenly

## Step 5
- while the ramen + mixuture is still covered, prepare the salad
- Add diced onion + coriander + spring onion/leek (optional) to the salad tomatoes in a small bowl
- cover with a small plate and vigorously shake to mix

## Step 6
- Add ramen to large bowl
- Top with the salad
- Drizzle some mustard sauce
- serve with a glass of chilled red grape juice
