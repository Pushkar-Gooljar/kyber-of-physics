# 1. The Null Hypothesis and the Alternative Hypothesis 
- When making a statistical enquiry, we often put forward a **hypothesis** (supposition) concerning the population parameter (usually the mean, $\mu$ or the proportion of success, $p$).
- The initial supposition is called the **null hypothesis** and is denoted by $\mathrm{H_{0}}$.
- $\mathrm{H_{0}}$ is tested against the **alternative hypothesis** $\mathrm{H_{1}}$ which may indicate a decrease ($<$), an increase ($>$) or a change
