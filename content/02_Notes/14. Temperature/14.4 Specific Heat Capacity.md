# 14.4 Specific Heat Capacity
>[!info] Definition: Specific Heat Capacity
>- Thermal energy per unit mass per unit change in temperature.

>[!math] Formula
>$$\large \Delta E = mc\Delta T$$
>
>Where:
>- $\Delta E$: Change in thermal energy ($\mathrm{J}$).
>- $m$: mass ($\mathrm{kg}$).
>- $c$: Specific heat capacity ($\mathrm{JKg^{-1}K^{-1}}$ or $\mathrm{JKg^{-1}{^\circ C}^{-1}}$).
>- $\Delta T$: Change in temperature ($\mathrm{K}$ or $\mathrm{^\circ C}$)
