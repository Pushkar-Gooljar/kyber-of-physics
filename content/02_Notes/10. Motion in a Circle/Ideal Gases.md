>[!info] How motion of molecules cause pressure exerted by gas
>>Explain how molecular movement causes the pressure exerted by a gas.
>>*9702_w24_qp_42-Q4(b)*
>
>- Molecules collide with walls of container
>- Momentum of molecule changes during collision with wall
>- According to Newton's Second Law, change in momentum is caused by force on molecule by wall
>- According to Newton's Third Law, molecule experiences  force from wall so molecule exerts force on wall
>- Many molecules exerting force across the area of the wall leads to pressure.
