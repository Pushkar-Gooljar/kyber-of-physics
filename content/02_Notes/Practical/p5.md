**To calculate $P(X=a,b,c,d,\dots)$ when $X\sim \operatorname{Po}(\lambda)$**
$$
P(X=a,b,c,d,\dots) = e^{-\lambda}\left( \frac{\lambda^a}{a!} +\frac{\lambda^b}{b!}+\frac{\lambda^c}{c!}+\frac{\lambda^d}{d!}+\dots \right)
$$

**Example: Finding $P(X=0,1,2)$ when $\lambda=4.5$**
1. Type the following and ensure cursor it at writing level afterwards (not in exponent)
$$
e^{-4.5}
$$
2. Press `SHIFT` + $\log_{x}Y$ (button below ON with a yellow summation sign above it)
3. Type the following:
$$
\frac{4.5^X}{X!}
$$
4. Press arrow up 2 times
5. Type the upper bound in this case it is 2
6. Press arrow down
7. Type the lower bound, in this case it is 0
8. It will calculate the appropriate value
