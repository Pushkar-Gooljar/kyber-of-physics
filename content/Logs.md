## Theoretical Equation
$$
\Large
\lg(T) = n\lg(L) + lg\left( \frac{2}{c} \right)
$$

## Equation from given headings/graph
$$
\Large
\lg(T/10^{-5}) = n\lg(L) + lg\left( \frac{2}{c} \right)
$$
## Step 1: Break up logs to remove powers of 10
$$
\Large
\begin{align}
\lg(T/10^{-5}) &= \lg T - \lg 10^{-5} \\
&= \lg T - (-5) \\
&= \lg T + 5 
\end{align}
$$
